FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

When should a business rebrand?
Consider rebranding when your business has outgrown its current identity, entered new markets, merged/acquired, or when your brand no longer reflects your values and direction.
Will rebranding confuse my existing customers?
Not if done strategically. We plan gradual transitions and maintain recognizable elements that preserve brand equity while signaling evolution.
How much does rebranding cost?
Rebranding projects start at our Comprehensive tier (₹18,999). Complex rebrands with extensive brand systems are quoted on a project basis.
How long does a rebrand take?
A strategic rebrand typically takes 2-4 weeks for the design phase, plus additional time for rollout planning and implementation support.
